Site Planning Document

Site Name

TravelSnap : The name TravelSnap conveys the idea of capturing travel moments (“snaps”) and sharing them in a simple, visual, and engaging way. It reflects the site’s focus on short travel stories, photos, and bite-sized destination guides.

Optional domain availability: wanderwelltravel.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of TravelSnap is to provide an easy-to-browse travel blog where visitors can explore destinations, view curated travel photos, and read short, helpful travel guides. The site will highlight user-friendly travel tips and visually engaging destinations while offering a simple contact form for feedback and inquiries.

Scenarios

  • “What are some good travel destinations and what do they look like?”
  • “Where can I get simple travel tips for planning my next trip?”
  • “How can I contact the blog owner for questions or collaboration?”

Color Schema (this may change)

  • Primary Color #2C6E8F (Deep Ocean Blue): Usage: Main headings, navigation bar background, call-to-action buttons.
  • Secondary Color – #F2C14E (Warm Sunset Gold): Usage: Accents, button hover states, section dividers, highlights.
  • Optional Neutral – #FFFFFF (White): Usage: Usage: Page background to keep readability clean.
  • Optional Accent – #333333 (Charcoal) Usage: Body text.

Typography

  • Heading Font – "Roboto", sans-serif: Sans serif font used for headings to evoke a premium feel.
  • Body Font – Roboto: Clean sans-serif font used for all paragraph and body content for easy readability.
